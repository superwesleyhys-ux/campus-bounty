## 1. Authority and bounded decisions

The source requirements are `docs/backend-source-plan.md` C3, E1 and E4: immutable original/effective terms, exact-version mutual consent, funding checks and preservation of submitted work. The implemented frozen team expands “both sides” into **publisher plus every original frozen collaborator**, individually. A later team roster does not change the agreement roster.

| Fact or operation | Authority today | Amendment contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original agreement | `agreements.terms`, protected by `immutable_agreement` (002); parties in `agreement_members` | Never overwrite original JSON, parties, IDs or timestamps. |
| Work identity and state | `milestones.id/version/revision`, exact submission/review links (001/010) | Keep IDs and work counters; add separate terms identity. |
| Date/criteria | 004: optional calendar target; immutable milestone criteria | Same date meaning; no automatic cancellation, penalty or retrospective requirement. |
| Application acceptance | `accept_task_invitation` (010), `guard_agreement_version` and `review_application_terms` (015) | Preserve existing current-listing consent gates; initialize agreement terms atomically. |
| Original-party consent | No amendment consent exists | Each required actor explicitly confirms the exact new revision. Creating/editing is **not** confirmation. |
| Work eligibility | 010 `lock_work_agreement`, active account/current affiliation, role-specific work rights | Preserve work policy. Amendment writes require active account/current affiliation and original-party role; do not introduce a new student requalification requirement for an existing agreement. |
| Payment coverage | No installed payment ledger or app-accessible funding setter | `active` is workflow state, not proof of money received. No new payment authority in this increment. |
| Agreement activation | Acceptance defaults to `awaiting_funding`; submission/review require `active` | Amendment confirmation cannot change activation state. |
| Fulfillment completion | Exact final acceptance makes agreement `completed` (010) | Completion is not settlement. Do not reopen completed agreements. |

Reversible product choices for this slice:

1. Any original party may propose. Only that proposal's author may revise or withdraw it; any original party may reject it. Everybody, including the author, confirms separately. This avoids captain proxy consent and edit ownership ambiguity, at the cost of requiring withdrawal/rejection and a new proposal when another person wants to take over.
2. At most one live proposal per agreement, including `agreed_waiting_funds`. A partial unique index enforces it. Rejection/withdrawal/staleness releases the slot. This avoids competing consensuses; alternative drafts remain local until the slot is available.
3. Dates, agreement description and milestone title/deliverable/criteria/amount are expressible. Permit adding future milestones and retiring only untouched pending milestones with no submission or review. Reward equals the sum of **effective included** milestone amounts; currency and parties stay fixed. Existing milestone IDs and positions are never deleted, reused or reassigned; new positions increase monotonically beyond every allocated position. Retired stages remain historical and cannot accept new work or count toward completion. Limit each effective/proposed included set to1–20 stages (the existing task bound), not total historical identities. Cost: history can grow and must paginate; scope expansion for a started stage needs a separately agreed follow-up stage.
4. A milestone with **any submitted version** keeps its title, amount, deliverable and criteria for its entire remaining lifecycle, including revision requests. Other milestones remain amendable. This protects performed work and avoids two possible amounts for acceptance of the same milestone. Added requirements for that work must be a new follow-up stage, leaving the old stage reviewable under its original criteria and amount. Cost: users see separate stages instead of a rewritten in-progress stage. Cancellation or reducing earned obligations remains a separate compensation policy.
5. Completed, cancelled and legacy agreement-level `submitted` states are read-only for amendments. The current work engine uses `active` plus milestone states; do not guess how to reopen legacy terminal/unsupported states. Pending proposals become stale if normal work completes the agreement.
6. All monetary/allocation changes can be proposed and unanimously agreed now, but cannot become effective until a real funds authority is integrated. Do not remove reward editing from the UI or label agreement on reward as funding.

These are implementation recommendations, not approval of payment/refund/compensation policy. The unresolved consequential policies are listed in section 5 and are gates on future funds execution, not on the safe proposal/consent/history flow.

## 3. Work, effective terms and ordinary concurrency

`agreements.terms` and physical `milestones` definition columns remain original. Read models explicitly expose original and effective definitions; do not silently replace the original `terms` field. Existing milestone state/version/revision/latest/accepted links continue to represent work state. New submission requests add `expectedEffectiveTermsId` and `expectedEffectiveRevision`, alongside existing `expectedVersion/expectedRevision`. Under the agreement lock, compare all four and bind the immutable submission to the current effective terms in the same transaction. A stale page returns409; it must not submit under a newly changed contract without user review.

Review and revision-request commands retain exact submission ID, work version and milestone revision, and additionally carry `submissionTermsId` and `submissionTermsRevision` matching its immutable association. **Do not compare review to today's effective revision.** Acceptance criteria, scope, target date and amount displayed for that review come from that submission's terms. Acceptance forms only settlement eligibility, never a transfer. A later resubmission binds current effective agreement terms but its already-started milestone retains the same scope/criteria/amount; the UI can show the later date/general description separately.

The effective terms set is also the current-stage authority: submission to a retired physical milestone returns409 `milestone_retired`; accepting an old submission cannot encounter a legally retired stage because retirement forbids any submitted work. IDs reserved only in a proposal have no executable milestone and return404 at work endpoints. No blanket work pause while a proposal is pending or agreed waiting for funds. Ordinary work continues under the current effective terms and existing active-state guard. If a proposal changes or retires an untouched milestone and that milestone gets a first submission before effectiveness, the proposal becomes `stale_work`; confirmations remain visible but cannot activate it. The submitting transaction appends the invalidation event and frees the live slot. If any other milestone is submitted/reviewed, the proposal need not become stale: the changed milestone set is what matters. All effectiveness paths independently recheck actual submissions and baseline definitions; a stale projection cannot bypass this check. For a global date/description-only change, old submissions remain under their old snapshot and later submissions use the new snapshot; no automatic adverse reinterpretation of old work.

If final acceptance completes the agreement before amendment effectiveness, transition its live amendment to `stale_agreement` in that transaction, including any proposed added stages. If an addition becomes effective first after valid real funds reconciliation, it prevents completion until that new stage is accepted. Retirement becomes effective only for a still-pending stage with no submissions/reviews; exclude it from work eligibility, budget and completion. If retirement leaves all remaining included stages already accepted, mark the agreement completed and append the ordinary completion event exactly once in that same effectiveness transaction. A new proposal cannot retire all stages: at least one included stage is required. Completion and manual completion must always join the current effective included set, never count candidate identities or retired rows. If effectiveness commits first, later submissions must match its revision. Serializing on the existing agreement lock gives one ordinary transaction order without forced scheduling. Existing work counters need not increment for a terms change: the new effective revision field supplies that guard. Never reuse `milestones.revision` as a terms revision.

Example (illustrative IDs, USD cents; not a real record): publisher P, original collaborators A (captain) and B. Original terms T1/revision1: target2027-03-01, M1=40000 for annotated data, M2=60000 for a report, total100000. In a synthetic-active isolated scenario, A submits S1 on M1 bound to T1; M1 is now permanently protected from scope/amount rewriting in this slice. B proposes Q/revision1/T2: target2027-03-15 and new report criteria on untouched M2, same amounts. P and A confirm; B has not, so Q is pending and T1 remains effective. P can review S1 against T1 throughout. When B explicitly confirms, T2 becomes effective; a later M2 submission binds T2. Accepting S1 still uses T1's M1 criteria/40000, even though the agreement header now shows T2. If A submits M2 under T1 before B confirms, Q becomes stale_work; that submission remains T1, and the proposal must be rebuilt without changing this started milestone. If Q instead adds follow-up M3=20000 for extra analysis (total120000), or raises untouched M2 to80000 (total120000), all three confirmations yield agreed_waiting_funds; T1 remains effective, no money is received, and there is no activation/payment button in this preview.

## 4. Commands, authorization and recovery

Reuse `store.as` actor context, `account`, `idempotent`, privateRead/RLS and BFF `api` current-session/request checks. `backend/http/endpoint.mjs` resolves the session actor, checks Host/Origin, applies the existing business rate limit, requires application/json and caps streamed bodies at64KiB; retain these limits and show draft-size validation before sending. The sum of per-field maximum lengths does not override this64KiB request cap. SQL must revalidate authenticated `campus.actor()`; actor IDs in request bodies are invalid. Reads and receipts never confer mutation authority.

Lock order for every agreement mutation: immutable resource locator/read visibility → agreement advisory lock `agreement:<uuid>` → agreement row → current actor user row FOR UPDATE then affiliation FOR SHARE (same current work policy) → terms-state row → amendment row if any → actor/operation/key idempotency lock → affected milestone rows in UUID order. This preserves the existing fulfillment ordering (authorization before key lookup, milestone lock inside the operation); any terms/amendment locks added to work operations must precede that same key lookup. Reentrant sanctioned calls take only already held locks. Use one shared helper for amendment operations, not `lock_work_agreement(...,'review')` to impersonate a publisher. Existing work and amendment helpers must use the same ordering. For reads that do not mutate, keep current privateRead semantics. Do not acquire application/task/team locks inside agreement mutation commands. New-accept initialization runs inside the existing task/application acceptance transaction and only initializes the newly created agreement; it must not take a conflicting multi-user agreement mutation path.

Before idempotency lookup, check visibility, current actor status/affiliation and role; repeat that check after lookup/key wait before returning the receipt. State/revision preconditions apply only to a fresh operation, so a legitimate completed receipt can be recovered after the state moved. Cross-agreement resource lookups return404. Domain errors preserve existing Problem envelope (`type,title,status,code,detail,requestId` through the existing handler); do not leak another agreement in conflict detail.

All writes use POST and Idempotency-Key (existing8–128 visible-ASCII limit). Receipt operation namespace includes resource ID: `agreement.amendment.propose:<agreementId>` or `amendment.<revise|confirm|reject|withdraw>:<amendmentId>`. Body hash includes full normalized terms, reason and all expected identities; server canonicalizes missing optional fields consistently before hashing. Same key/different body409. Stable business uniqueness is additional to key uniqueness. Return immutable receipt separately from a freshly authorized current read model.

Moving cents between milestones, adding a positive-budget stage or retiring a positive-budget stage is monetary even with net delta0. Structural changes therefore reach agreed_waiting_funds now and require the same real authority before effectiveness; they are not hidden from the proposal journey. ## 6. Legacy provenance and new acceptance

### Exact cent allocation and fee variance

The following **technical allocation proposal `charge_member_stage_v1`** is selected for design review and must be displayed and included in exact quote/contract consent before execution. It implements the confirmed proportions; the user did not specify this cent/lot mapping. Rationale: each charge bears its own fee, members retain their confirmed charge-level fee shares, and no implementation can front-load the entire fee into the first released stage. Cost: very small top-ups can fail the per-component nonnegative-net check, and changing the allocation algorithm requires a new exact prospective plan rather than a silent recomputation.

`LR(A, weights, keys)` uses integers only: for total nonnegative cents A and nonnegative integer weights with positive sum W, assign `floor(A*w/W)`, then distribute the remaining cents in descending `(A*w mod W)` order, with ascending immutable key breaking ties. Zero weights receive zero and are excluded from remainder recipients. A=0 produces all zero; positive A with no positive weight is invalid. Team ratio units sum exactly to 10000, with user UUID as member key. Stage key is `(permanent position, milestone UUID)`; no display order, arrival timestamp, provider event order or floating point participates.

For each planned charge lot L, allocate an internal `fundingPlanLotId` **before** provider dispatch; bind the eventual single accepted charge to it. Its immutable principal plan names each covered stage and exact member gross `g[L,m,s]`; sum over members/stages equals `P[L]`. Initial full-upfront funding uses one lot: apply LR to each signed stage gross with the confirmed team ratios. Repeated attempts are not new lots unless the old attempt is authoritatively terminal and the obligation still unsatisfied; accidental extra success is suspense, not a second allocation of the same stage.

For each charge's consented estimated fee `F[L]`, propose `B[L]=ceil(F[L]/2)` for publisher fee contribution and `R[L]=floor(F[L]/2)` for recipient fee. The one odd cent is visibly assigned to the publisher; no remainder is platform revenue. `memberFee[L,m]=LR(R[L], confirmed team ratios, user UUIDs)[m]`. Then allocate **that member's entire fee for that lot** over only that lot's funded stages: `f[L,m,*]=LR(memberFee[L,m], g[L,m,*], stage keys)`. Net component is `n[L,m,s]=g[L,m,s]-f[L,m,s]`. A zero-gross member/lot with a positive assigned fee, or any f>g, is `422 fee_exceeds_component`; reject the executable quote/assessment, do not borrow another member's gross or an earlier charge's money. Zero gross with zero fee is valid; zero net is displayed and produces no zero-value transfer. Fees are never reallocated based on which stage happens to be accepted first.

Persist and consent to every `{fundingPlanLotId,chargeId when known,transactionTermsId/revision,allocationRevision,calculationVersion,memberId,milestoneId,stagePosition,grossCents,recipientFeeCents,netCents,publisherFeeContributionCents at lot level,sourcePlanRevision}`. The provider charge ID is a later verified mapping, not a reason to alter the signed pre-dispatch component amounts. Composite FKs and controlled sum checks enforce: `Σg=P`, `Σm memberFee=R`, `Σs f[L,m,s]=memberFee[L,m]`, `Σm,s f=R`, `Σm,s n=P-R`, `B+R=F`, and each stage's gross across its approved lots equals its exact funded obligation without overfunding. Payables and transfer operations name the exact component ID. Final release sums these existing net components; it must not recompute fees/rounding on one aggregate payout. One eligible member/stage can therefore have separate transfer components for different source charges.

**Two-stage worked plan (illustrative cents, no Stripe pricing claim):** one full-upfront lot, stages S1=10000 and S2=10000, team A/B=60%/40%, fee F=600. Publisher contribution B=300, recipient fee R=300; member fees A=180, B=120. The member-to-stage LR allocation is:

| Lot / stage | A gross | A fee | A net | B gross | B fee | B net | Stage net |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L1 / S1 | 6000 | 90 | 5910 | 4000 | 60 | 3940 | 9850 |
| L1 / S2 | 6000 | 90 | 5910 | 4000 | 60 | 3940 | 9850 |
| L1 total | 12000 | 180 | 11820 | 8000 | 120 | 7880 | 19700 |

Charge total is 20300, processor fee 600, net cash 19700. Per-stage release sends 5910/3940 after S1 and again after S2; final release sends sums 11820/7880 only after completion, with the same source-component ledger. Neither path charges all 180/120 against S1. Tie example: two one-cent stages for one member with a one-cent recipient fee allocates that fee to the lower stage key, producing stage nets 0 and 1; a total recipient fee of 3 against only 2 gross cents rejects the quote.

**Multiple lots and amendments:** do not pool fees and prorate again after payments occur. An approved prospective top-up has a new lot/charge, its own publisher/recipient fee and per-member/per-stage map. Derive its gross increments from the newly consented exact stage/member gross targets minus already assigned protected/base gross components; negative increments require a separate reallocation assessment and cannot be financed by a new charge. Allocate only positive uncovered increments, in permanent stage/member identity order, without assigning principal twice. Changes to an existing lot are possible only through a new fully consented source-plan revision before any affected component has been submitted, earned, fee-consumed or reserved/dispatched; all charge/member totals must still hold and old revisions remain immutable. Otherwise that lot's components are fixed. In particular this design consumes an actual matched charge's fee components once before release, so later amendments cannot move those sunk fees to new stages, a different member or a different charge. A requested reallocation/decrease unable to preserve those fixed components stays `agreed_waiting_funds`/blocked for the separately approved compensation policy; it is not silently normalized.

Example: after L1/S1 is settled, add untouched S3=10000 by new lot L2 with illustrative F=300, B=150, R=150; A/B gross=6000/4000, fees=90/60, nets=5910/3940. L1/S2 still pays its original 5910/3940. Across L1+L2: principal 30000, publisher contribution 450, charge receipts 30450, actual matched processing fees 900, recipient withholding 450 and net transfers 29550; no L2 fee is charged to S1/S2. If a permitted top-up covers an untouched existing stage, its incremental lot component is separately displayed and added to the original component, never a recalculation of the old lot. Started stages remain protected by the agreement contract; fee-allocation consent cannot override that prohibition. This fixes normal top-up mapping without approving refund/decrease/fee-variance outcomes.

A final quote stores `{principalCents,platformServiceFeeCents:0,estimatedProcessorFeeCents,publisherProcessorFeeCents,recipientProcessorFeeCents,memberBreakdown,sourceComponentPlan,calculationVersion:"charge_member_stage_v1",chargeTotalCents,feeEstimateSource,feeCalculationVersion,roundingRule,feeAdjustmentPolicyId,quoteDigest}`. The fee estimate must be calculated for the **actual total charge**, including the publisher share; do not estimate on principal and then increase the charge without recomputing. Method-dependent fees must use the selected approved configuration/rate model or wait for a final quote; never claim the illustrative amount is a provider quote. Actual fee comes from the relevant provider balance transaction; unavailable fee means `fee_actual_pending`, not zero. Until actual fee matches this consented plan or an independently approved exact adjustment is in place, the estimated component plan is reserved/provisional and cannot authorize transfers. Matching actual fee promotes the exact plan; it does not calculate a different stage allocation.

If actual fee equals the accepted estimate, exact approved accounting can proceed. If different, record `fee_variance_pending` and present actual total, the 50/50 recalculation, each member difference and the permitted next action. Do not autocharge the publisher, deduct extra from payees, invent platform absorption, or promise automatic refunds. Keep the affected settlement component held until an approved adjustment mechanism plus any required new exact consent exists. This preserves the confirmed ratio while leaving the still-undecided adjustment policy explicit. Already paid allocations are never rewritten. Fee refunds/rebates and chargeback/reversal costs have their own approved treatment, not an assumed extension of the ordinary processing fee rule.

## 5. Money state, journal rules and release algorithm

Payment attempts: `creating → requires_action/processing → succeeded|failed|canceled`; any indeterminate external outcome → `unknown`. Failure is terminal only when verified provider state says no successful charge remains possible; a timeout is not failed. Checkout completed while unpaid remains processing. Expired session alone is not proof a previously initiated asynchronous payment failed. Record a succeeded charge forever; refund/dispute changes separate state.

### Principal capacity, fee recovery and balanced settlement (I2)

For a normal lot without tax/shipping or compensation, receipt `C=P+B` is split into gross reward principal P and publisher fee contribution B; it is not all a reward liability. Consent also partitions each gross component g into recipient-fee component f and net component n. On verified receipt reserve B and every f for processing cost, identified separately from any transfer/refund reservation. Thus the fee portions are already excluded from spendable principal before acceptance. Matching actual fee F=B+Σf permits one atomic fee-consumption posting: consume both fee-share reservations, discharge the corresponding liabilities and record cost recovery. Never count cost recovery as platform service-fee revenue or unexplained retained cash. Platform service fee remains 0.

For each source member/stage component, define uncommitted principal headroom as `g + approved principal restorations - feeReservedOrConsumed - netTransferred - principalRefunded - activeNetTransferReservation - activePrincipalRefundReservation - otherPrincipalHold`. `feeReservedOrConsumed` is a disjoint union: a fee component is reserved **or** consumed, never both; successful transfer/refund similarly replaces its active reservation. Holds are on remaining cents only, not an extra subtraction of already reserved money. This headroom is an upper bound on allocation, not refund entitlement or release permission. Publisher B is in its own fee-contribution bucket, never transferable reward. Any approved refund of it requires its own liability/fee treatment and cannot be taken from principal automatically. Transfers draw only eligible net n; each component and lot must reconcile independently.

Separately cap new commitments by verified provider-available cash for the source/environment, less active transfer/refund cash reservations and disjoint liquidity holds. For an as-yet undebited known fee, reserve its cash requirement; once the fee debit is observed, replace that requirement with the actual cash reduction rather than subtracting the same fee twice. `fee_actual_pending` or unexplained variance holds the lot's entire remaining settlement capacity regardless of apparent gross clearing balance. A successful transfer plus recipient fee **consumption** satisfies the accepted gross amount g in normal settlement; a reservation, pending fee or earlier deduction by itself does not mean work accepted or gross payable settled. Initially full coverage means P was actually prepaid and allocated to the agreed net and fee components, not that the provider must still hold P cash after charging F. Completed earned gross must not be recollected as a shortfall merely because its agreed fee was consumed.

Dr/Cr names are control-ledger accounts, not a claim about statutory accounting or tax treatment. Processing-cost recovery is a contra-expense account, separate from platform-service-fee revenue.

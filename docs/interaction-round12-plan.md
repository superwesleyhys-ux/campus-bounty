## Product behavior and acceptance contract

Owners can edit a published or paused bounty/product, review their proposed changes, save a new immutable version, pause public display/new applications or purchases, and republish. Signed-agreement fulfillment and existing orders continue with their existing immutable terms; pause is not cancellation, refund or deletion. Contracted bounties cannot be reopened for another agreement through this control. UI explains these consequences before action, shows current visibility and revision, and offers owner management from My bounties/My listings and exact detail.

Bounty editing includes title/category/description/reward/target delivery date and milestone deliverables/budgets/acceptance criteria under existing validation, including total budget equality. Product editing includes title/description/price/delivery/shipping and available stock under existing limits. Avoid only permitting edits before any application/order; that is not the requested complete lifecycle. No new automatic moderation, compensation or release policy is inferred.

Published terms are versioned and immutable. New applications bind the displayed terms version; submitted proposal and frozen team membership are not rewritten. If listing terms change during an active application/invitation, participants can see their prior terms and current changes, explicitly review the new version, and reaffirm consent for that exact version. Every required frozen member and the applicant must affirm current terms before a fresh publisher invitation for that version can be accepted. Old invitations cannot silently accept new terms. Another edit invalidates only stale acknowledgements/invitation authority, not historical records. Ordinary withdrawal/rejection, chat and accurate notifications continue to work. No auto-consent, auto-application or implicit acceptance.

Existing applications predate version storage. Record migration-time snapshots as observed current terms, not recovered historical submission facts. Preserve old signed agreements exactly; label unknown legacy provenance honestly. Define and test the legacy active-application transition to explicit current-version review when needed, without silently rewriting their proposal/member records or manufacturing past consent.

Product versions preserve prior listing terms. Existing orders retain their price/title/delivery/terms and reservation obligation. A stale unconsumed quote must reject after relevant edit/pause; a previously consumed quote still returns the same authorized existing order through established idempotency. Pausing cannot silently cancel a reservation or prevent its normal cancellation/expiry stock release. Available-stock edits use row locking plus an explicit expected stock/revision contract so concurrent reservations/releases cannot be overwritten.

Owners retain management access to paused listings. Public catalogs exclude paused listings. Saved and already-authorized history users retain honest unavailable/paused history rather than a broken or misleading buy/apply control; direct resource access obeys the same authorization design. Account/eligibility checks are enforced on server, not just hidden controls. Notifications and links for affected current participants identify the right listing/version and retain page context, without leaking to outsiders.

All new writes use explicit expected revisions, actor/operation-scoped idempotency and audit events. Stale forms preserve drafts and require deliberate reload/review before retry. Unknown network outcomes preserve the original request key/body and can read back/recover; do not blindly create a second version or resubmit. No misleading success on a stale/replayed receipt; distinguish original receipt from current state. Drafts are actor+listing scoped. Accessible confirmations, loading/error/success states and focus at390/1440.
